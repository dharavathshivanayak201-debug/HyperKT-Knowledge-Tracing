# Contributions and research changes

Keep corrections to documentation or packaging separate from new experiments. Label new training runs with their own protocol, configuration, seeds, dataset hashes, selection rule and results; do not replace original tables or tune on the held-out test set.

- **Evidence:** provide actual logs and artifact hashes; never manufacture missing runs or replace negative findings with aspirational claims.
- **Privacy:** do not submit interactions, source learner IDs, split-ID lists, prediction rows, optimizer states or nested archives containing them.
- **Claims:** causal prerequisite, calibrated uncertainty, official-baseline reproduction and multi-dataset statements require new evidence.
- **Licensing:** the absence of a license is intentional pending the rights holder's decision, not an invitation to assume one.
